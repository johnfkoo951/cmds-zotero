- Annotations sidebar: colour chips with counts, a filter menu (type, tag, attachment, with or without comment), Clear, and sort (page, reading order, last modified, colour) and grouping (attachment, page, colour) controls. Filters only show or hide cards that are already built; Korean text matches in composed and decomposed forms.
- Annotations sidebar modes: follow the active note, the item selected in Zotero (read when you ask, never by a listener), the citekey at the cursor, or a pinned item. The mode, pinned item, filters, sort and grouping are restored with the workspace. A source with nothing to show is explained in place. New commands: follow the active note, show the item selected in Zotero, follow the citekey at the cursor, pin the shown item, pin an item, unpin.
- Annotations sidebar: Fetch latest (button and command) reads the shown item from Zotero once and shows its current annotations until the index changes; the header says "Live from Zotero · HH:MM" or the saved snapshot's age. The saved index is not rewritten, the sidebar still opens from the snapshot without waiting for Zotero, and a failed read keeps the snapshot with a Notice saying why.
- Annotation cards: a More actions menu (also on right-click) to copy the annotation as a citation (`[@citekey, p. label]`, optionally with its Zotero link), copy the callout, the bibliography entry, the annotation or item key (`<libraryID>:<key>` in group libraries) or the rename-safe identity link, and to show the annotation's data read-only. The same actions are palette commands for the card last clicked in the sidebar.
- Opening a PDF without asking for a page (the item actions' Open PDF, the references sidebar's Open PDF, and identity links to a PDF without a page) now opens it where you stopped reading in Zotero instead of on page 1. Open source PDF at page, page links and annotation links keep their page.
- Search annotations (command): searches every indexed annotation by text, comment, tag, page, colour, citekey or title (word by word; Korean matches in composed and decomposed forms) without asking Zotero; the box shows the snapshot's age. Enter inserts the callout, Shift+Enter a quote ending in `[@citekey, p. label]` with its Zotero link, Mod+Enter only the link, Alt+Enter pins the item in the annotations sidebar. Image and ink annotations insert links in the quote format.
